The Ceremony

What is a Quaker wedding ceremony?

Quaker ceremonies are a little different from traditional weddings. There won’t be an officiant or a set program. Instead, we’ll gather in silence, exchange vows with one another, and invite those present into a period of shared reflection. Anyone who feels moved may stand and offer a brief message, story, blessing, or reflection. The ceremony typically lasts about 45 minutes.

What if I want to participate in the ceremony?

Quaker ceremonies are built around the idea that everyone present helps create the experience. During this time, anyone who feels moved may stand and share a brief message, reflection, story, or blessing. There’s no expectation to speak, and many people choose not to, but we welcome contributions offered from the heart.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We kindly ask that guests refrain from taking photos during the ceremony and instead join us fully in the moment. Once the celebration begins afterward, we’d love to see all the photos.

Can I bring my children?

Children are warmly welcome at the reception. Our ceremony, however, will be adults-only. Because we’re having a “Quaker-style” ceremony centered around quiet reflection and participation, we felt an adults-only ceremony would create the best experience for everyone.

Getting There & Staying

Is parking available?

Yes! Complimentary valet parking will be provided. That said, we strongly encourage guests to carpool or rideshare if possible.

Where should I stay?

We don’t have a hotel block, but there are great options throughout Marin County. For convenience, we recommend staying in Fairfax, San Anselmo, or San Rafael. If you’d like to turn the trip into a longer getaway, Mill Valley, Sausalito, and Point Reyes are all wonderful options.

A few hotels to consider:

  • AC Hotel San Rafael Downtown
  • Marin Lodge
  • Embassy Suites by Hilton
  • The Lodge at Tiburon
Which airport should I fly into?

We recommend flying into either San Francisco International (SFO) or Oakland International (OAK). Both are approximately one hour from the venue, depending on traffic.

If you’re planning to rent a car, either airport works well. If you’re deciding between flights, we generally suggest choosing whichever option is more convenient or affordable.
